We do one thing right

Al Pastor

Marinated pork, carved straight off the trompo. Here's how to eat it.

  • Tacos al pastor

    Freshly marinated pork cooked on the spit, layer by layer, and carved straight off the trompo with fresh pineapple, onion and cilantro. On corn or flour tortillas.

  • Gringa

    Al pastor and melted cheese on a flour tortilla.

  • Salsas

    A lineup of house salsas, red to green, to load up every taco.

  • Quesadilla

    Melted cheese, folded into a griddled tortilla.
